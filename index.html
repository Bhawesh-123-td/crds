Who we are

Community development rooted in local priorities.

CRDS Nepal was established in 1992 in Darchula District. It began with a focus on operating a secondary school and has evolved into a social development organization implementing diverse programs across geographical areas and populations in Nepal.

The organization focuses on improving services and access to rights in education, health, livelihoods and humanitarian needs while strengthening accountability, inclusion and resilience.

Vision

Social Justice based Prosperous and Equitable Society.

Mission

Facilitate the agency of marginalized, poor and vulnerable communities so they can exercise fundamental rights and access services while strengthening accountability and equitable development.

Goal

Increase access to quality education, accessible health systems, climate-responsive development and equitable livelihoods.
